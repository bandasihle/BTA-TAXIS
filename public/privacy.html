Privacy

What Dankie Driver collects, and why

Who is responsible

The Benoni Taxi Association runs Dankie Driver and is responsible for the information in it, under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated 28 November 2026

Passengers

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no cookies.
  • Your phone keeps the last fares it saw, so they show without signal. That copy stays on your phone.
  • The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which sees your internet address when it sends them.

Drivers: your taxi's location

  • Your phone sends its position only after you agree to the location notice in the app, and only between pressing Start and Stop, while your taxi is on a trip.
  • Passengers and association staff see your taxi's number plate and position, never your name.
  • Only the latest position is kept, never a history of where you went. It is deleted when you press Stop, when the taxi arrives or goes off duty, or when it stops sending for 10 minutes.
  • You can withdraw your agreement in the app at any time. Your position is deleted straight away.

Staff accounts

  • For drivers, rank marshals and the office: your name, cellphone number, role, and your rank or taxi.
  • Your PIN is stored scrambled, so nobody can read it, not even the association.
  • When you last signed in, and wrong PIN attempts, to protect your account.
  • What marshals record at a rank (check-ins, seats, departures) is kept with the marshal's name.

Your rights

Nothing is sold or given to anyone else. You can ask the association what information it holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted.

To ask, speak to the association office.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
