How to install it

  1. Tap Download above and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Android warns about apps that don't come from the Play Store. That is expected for now: the app will be on the Play Store soon. Only download it from this page.

Updating

When a new version is out, the app tells you. Download it here again and install it over the old one. You stay signed in.

Catching a taxi?

Passengers don't need this app. Find ranks, fares and taxis on the way on the website, and add it to your home screen.
